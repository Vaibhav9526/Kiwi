ZAPI — Voice-First AI Inside KIWI Mail

An AI assistant built into the mail client that runs on voice commands — send mail, reach any feature, and clear the inbox without touching the keyboard.

What Zapi Does

  • Send mail by voice — "Reply to Sam: I'll be there at 5" — dictated, composed, sent
  • Summarizes all your mail — one command turns a full inbox into a spoken brief
  • Auto-replies to typical mail — routine questions answered without you
  • Deletes useless mail — noise gone on command, inbox stays clean
  • Every feature by voice — search, open, archive, flag — the whole client, hands-free

Why It Matters

  • Hands-free triage: inbox zero while you cook, drive, or code — mail stops being a screen-bound chore.
  • Accessibility first: the whole mailbox stays usable when typing is hard or impossible.
  • Attention back: summaries replace scrolling; auto-replies kill routine interruptions.
  • Zero context switch: speak a command from anywhere in the app — no menus, no shortcuts to memorize.
